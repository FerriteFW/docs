### Left out, and why

Settings seen in stock firmware, CHIRP or other radios that Ferrite doesn't offer.

| Setting | Seen in | Why not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice prompts, language | Stock, CHIRP | Non-goals: no voice prompts, English only |
| Key assignment (PF1, PF2, long-press digits) | Stock, CHIRP | Ferrite has one fixed key map |
| FHSS, encryption | Stock, CHIRP | Not legal on amateur bands in most places, and not in scope. The voice scrambler is different: it comes later, for use where it is legal (decision 82) |
| Remote kill, stun, wake | CHIRP | Commercial fleet features; a radio that can be disabled over the air is a hazard |
| SOS remote alarm | Stock | Transmits unattended. A local siren or flashlight may come later; nothing that keys the transmitter |
| LCD contrast, screen rotation | CHIRP | The panel needs neither; portrait only |
| Dealer and factory locks | CHIRP | No product lock-in; transmit limits are the band plan in code |
| Lock ranges per band | CHIRP | The band plan in code and the calibration windows already decide this |
| ARTS, EPCS pager | Yaesu | Yaesu-specific protocols |
| Battery type | CHIRP | One battery model; the trim covers reading errors |
| Auto power on at a time | CHIRP | Needs RTC-alarm and power-latch hardware not found on the board |
| Per-channel TOT | RT-880G | One TX timeout keeps the safety rule simple |
| Bell on call | CHIRP | DTMF and selcall decodes already toast and log |
| Work band, RX modulation, zone or channel mode, MDF-A/B/C | Stock | Replaced by band from frequency, per-channel mode, and the V/M key and channel display setting |
| Comment field per channel | — | None, on the radio or in the web app (decided Oct 6, 2026) |
