## Appendix G. Link protocol

The wire format of chapter 29 (LINK-1, LINK-21 – LINK-23), as built Oct 6, 2026. **Protocol 1** (decision 385, Ferrite 0.8.5): the number Ferrite 0.9 first released. Until 0.9 the format changed without a new number. From 0.9 on, any change to the wire format raises the number (decision 401); nothing older is kept, and a client refuses a radio that says another number (Console then offers only the update). Every message and record below is in every radio; the one capability byte (LINK-35.2) says only what differs by build or session. (0.8.4 and before said 3.) Shared code: `ferrite-core` `link.rs`; the radio's side: `ferrite-app` `link.rs`; a PC reference: `ferrite/tools/ferrite_link.py`.

**Frames.** A frame on the wire is its body COBS-encoded, then a 0x00 delimiter (0x00 never appears inside). The body is `type`, `seq`, `payload` (up to 240 bytes), then the CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE (poly 0x1021, initial 0xFFFF) of type, seq and payload, little-endian. The PC sends a 0x00 first to take the cable (TERM-2). On Bluetooth the same bytes go over the BT513's FFE0 service, characteristic FFE1: writes (without response) of at most 20 bytes, paced for the module's 9600-baud UART (about 0.8 KB/s), and the answers as notifications in any chunking; FF31 is never used. The cable and Bluetooth each have their own session (LINK-28); a write while the other port's session is mid-write answers `busy`. Every request (type 0x01–0x7F) is answered with `type | 0x80`, the same `seq`, and a payload that starts with a status byte. Numbers are little-endian.

A station: flags (u8: 1 a position follows, 2 course and speed, 4 altitude), callsign (length, text), symbol (u8: 0 car, 1 home, 2 digi, 3 weather, 4 handheld, 5 iGate, 6 truck, 7 boat), age (u32, seconds), times heard (u16), then the latitude and longitude (2 × i32, degrees × 1e7), course (u16, degrees) and speed (u16, km/h), altitude (i32, m) as the flags say, the path (length, text: the digipeaters, the used one marked `*`; empty when heard direct), the comment (length, text, up to 40). A message: in (u8: 1 to us, 0 ours), state (u8: 0 unread, 1 read, 2 pending, 3 acked, 4 retrying, 5 failed, 6 rejected: APRS-34; a reader shows a value it doesn't know as unknown), tries (u8, while retrying), age (u32, seconds), who (length, text: from, or to), the text (length, text).

**The radio state** (`STATE`, LINK-67; little-endian): the radio's part, 16 bytes: version (u8, 1), the active slot (u8), flags (u8: 1 transmitting, 2 Dry TX on, 4 the keys locked, 8 the battery low), battery (u8, %), battery (u16, mV, to 10 mV), GPS (u8: 0 off, 1 searching, 2 a fix), satellites used (u8), latitude and longitude (i32 each, degrees x 1e7; 0x7FFFFFFF both without a position). Then each slot's part, A to C, 29 bytes: flags (u8: 1 on a channel, 2 scanning, 4 squelch open, 8 transmitting, 16 the APRS slot, 32 plays the broadcast, 64 muted, 128 off), channel (u16, 0-based; 0xFFFF on its VFO), zone (u16; 0xFFFF on a VFO, 0xFFFE Local), frequency (u32, 10 Hz: what it receives now), mode (u8: 0 FM, 1 NFM, 2 AM, 6 WFM), power (u8: 0 low, 1 mid, 2 high), signal (i16, dBm, while open; −32768 else), name (16 bytes, 0-padded: the channel's, or the broadcast's RDS station name).

**Events** (`EVENT`'s payload, LINK-46): kind 1 a station heard: the heard list's count (u8), the station. Kind 2 a new message, in or out: the count (u16), the message. Kind 3 a message's state changed: the count (u16), the message as it is now. Kind 4 Live View: below. Kind 5 an event logged (LINK-57): the record as `LOG_READ` gives it (below), its sequence number first; oldest first, each once. Kind 6 the radio state (LINK-67.1): a parts mask (u8: 1 the radio's part, 2, 4, 8 slots A, B, C), then those parts in that order as the record below lays them out; the first after a subscription has them all (15). Kind 7 the lists changed (LINK-71): what (u8: 2 channels, 3 zones, 4 range presets), then the lists hash (u32) as `HELLO` ends with it; sent once writing has been quiet 500 ms (one for a `WRITE_MANY` of many records), one list an event.

**Live View** (kind 4, LINK-47, LINK-48; shared code: `ferrite-core` `liveview.rs`, a client in `tools/ferrite_link.py`'s `LiveView`). The compact form is the `UI` readback's lines (up to 48) less `state`'s `frame=` and `ago=`, each line cut after its last whole token that fits 230 coded bytes. A line's tokens are split at the spaces outside double quotes (inside them a backslash escapes the next byte); joining them with one space gives the line. Text on the wire is coded: a byte 0x01-0x1F stands for that entry of the dictionary (version 1), any other byte for itself:

| | | | | | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 `row␣` | 2 `"␣"` | 3 `␣sel` | 4 `␣dot` | 5 `␣grey` | 6 `card␣` | 7 `␣active=` | 8 `␣head="` |
| 9 `"␣state="` | 10 `␣freq=` | 11 `␣name="` | 12 `"␣mode="` | 13 `"␣tone="` | 14 `"␣shift="` | 15 `"␣power="` | 16 `"␣s="S` |
| 17 `"␣meter=` | 18 `␣rx=` | 19 `␣scan=` | 20 `␣kind=slot` | 21 `hints␣"` | 22 `screen␣` | 23 `"␣title="` | 24 `bar␣lock=` |
| 25 `␣watch=` | 26 `␣inbox=` | 27 `␣battery=` | 28 `␣clock=` | 29 `state␣dark=` | 30 `␣keyed=` | 31 `qmenu␣"` | |

(␣ is a space.) An update: seq (u8), flags (u8: 1 the whole state: drop every line first; 2 more follows soon: the check isn't valid yet), check (u16: FNV-1a 32 over every line, each followed by `\n`, its low 16 bits), lines (u8: the count after; new lines start empty), then ops to the end, each led by a byte whose top two bits are the op and the low six the line:

- 0 LINE: length (u8), the line, coded.
- 1 TOKENS: the line's token count after (u8), entries (u8), then each entry: token index (u8; bit 7: the value only, the token's key up to its first `=` is the one there), length (u8), the token or the value, coded. Tokens past the count go; new ones start empty.
- 2 TOKENS with the count unchanged: as 1 without the count byte.

A client applies an update only if it carries the whole state or the seq after the last one it applied, then, unless more follows, checks its lines against the check; otherwise (a lost or late update, a mismatch) it sends `SUBSCRIBE` without renew and gets the whole state. The radio diffs against what it last sent this session (1 KB of the lines' text, longer screens' lines by hash and then sent whole), so an update is exact. A meter moving on one card is one op of 11 bytes (op 2 on the card's line, two entries: `s=`'s value `"S3"` and `meter=`'s), 17 with the kind and header, 23 on the wire.

**Layouts and the update** (LINK-59 – LINK-62, UPD-16, decision 333). What Console needs to move a radio's data from layout 2 to layout 3 with `DATA_READ` and `DATA_WRITE` alone; shared code: `ferrite-core` `layout.rs` (the map), `ferrite-store` `records.rs` (the records), `store.rs` (the header), `logs.rs` (the rings). Little-endian throughout; CRC-16 is CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE (poly 0x1021, init 0xFFFF, no reflection, no final XOR), CRC-32 is CRC-32/ISO-HDLC (as `DATA_READ`'s).

Layout 3, the chip by address (4 KB sectors; never written: calibration and the update area):

The layout header (20 bytes at 0x000000, the rest of its sector erased): magic (u32 0x54525246, the bytes `46 52 52 54`), layout (u16: 3), 10 installer bytes (erased, 0xFF; bytes 6-9 later hold the calibration sector's CRC-32, which the firmware programs into them, CAL-7), CRC-32 (u32) of bytes 0-15 with bytes 6-9 taken as 0xFF. From byte 0x100 of its sector: the calibration CRCs the owner accepted since (CAL-20.3), 4 bytes each, the last one counting. Layout 2 had the same header at 0x02C000 with layout 2. The radio writes it, never Console (raw writes to it are refused): the format, or the begin before a power-on's first raw write (decision 339).

The migration mark (decision 339; 16-byte records at 0x00E000, 256 places, raw writes refused): magic (u32 0x564D5246, the bytes `46 52 4D 56`), the layout found (u16), this layout (u16: 3), 4 bytes erased, CRC-32 of bytes 0-11. Before the first raw write of a power-on the radio programs one into the first erased place (none left: the sector erased first) unless a valid one is there, then the layout 3 header at 0x000000 unless a good one is there (that sector erased first if it holds anything else), each read back. A valid mark means a move not stamped: the radio waits for Console (UPD-16) whatever the header says, and says the chip's layout as 255. `STAMP` checks the header and clears the mark, programming its 16 bytes to zero (no erase), taken only once the store has nothing queued or being written. So the chip always holds the old header, or the mark, or (stamped) the new header and all the data.

The channel record (64 bytes; channel n at 0x010000 + (n / 63) × 4096 + (n % 63) × 64; each sector's last 64 bytes free): 0 RX (u32, 10 Hz; 0xFFFFFFFF: empty), 4 TX (u32: 0 the same as RX; else the absolute TX frequency, a split when flag bit 10 is set), 8 mode (0 FM, 1 NFM, 2 AM, 3-5 HF modes, 6 WFM, 7 NAM; CHAN-23, CHAN-26), 9 narrow (0, 1), 10 power + 1 (0 default), 11 step + 1 (0 the band's), 12 RX tone (u16, CHAN-3), 14 TX tone (u16), 16 flags (u16: bits 0 skip, 1 APRS, 2 RX only, 3 busy lockout, 5 talkaround, 6 hidden, 7 has position, 8 priority, 9 reverse, 10 split; 4 and 11-15 kept as they are), 18 the zone's low byte, 19 squelch (0 Global, 1-10 levels 0-9), 20 latitude and 24 longitude (i32, 1e-7 degree; 0x7FFFFFFF none), 28-30 kept, 31 end tone (bits 0-2) and tail (bits 3-4), 32 name (16 bytes, 0-padded), 48-59 kept (48 is 0 in a new record, 49-59 0xFF), 60 version (2), 61 the zone's high byte, 62 CRC-16 (u16) of bytes 0-61. The zone is u16 0-511, 0xFFFF none. It holds a channel when the RX isn't 0xFFFFFFFF, the CRC agrees, byte 60 is 2 and the zone is 0-511 or 0xFFFF. Version 1 (layout 2): byte 60 1, byte 61 0xFF, the zone the one byte 18 (0-253, 255 none).

The zone record (32 bytes; zone z at 0x050000 + (z / 127) × 4096 + (z % 127) × 32): 0 name (16, 0-padded), 16 flags (bit 0 scan all, 1 shown in ◀▶, 2 sort by distance), 17 resume (0 Global, 1-10 s, 11 carrier drop, 12 stop on hit), 18 dwell, 19 0, 20 priority 1 and 22 priority 2 (u16 channel numbers, 0xFFFF none), 24 the channel count (u16, CHAN-36; 0xFFFF in layout 2), 26-29 0xFF, 30 CRC-16 of bytes 0-29. Empty when bytes 0-29 are all 0xFF or the CRC disagrees. Zones exist from 0 up to the last record held (a deleted earlier one stays as an empty, unnamed zone).

Layout 2 to 3 (Console, from its copy of 0x02C000-0x08CFFF and layout 2's scratch ring 0x1E0000-0x1FFFFF). First, layout 2's own recovery: a scratch sector whose last 32 bytes (at offset 4064) start with a valid footer (magic u32 0x52435346, the target address u32, the CRC-32 of the sector's first 4064 bytes, a CRC-16 of those 12 footer bytes) has its 4064 bytes put over its target sector in the copy. Then, region by region (each layout 3 region erased whole, then its bytes programmed; 64-byte pieces that are all 0xFF can be skipped):

| Layout 2 | Layout 3 | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Header 0x02C000 | 0x000000 | Not copied: the radio writes layout 3's before the first raw write, behind the migration mark; `STAMP` clears the mark, last |

| Zones 0x03F000-0x040FFF (0-253) | 0x050000-0x050FFF | Each record held: bytes 24-25 its channel count after the translation, the CRC again; at the same offset. | Presets and DTMF 0x041000 | 0x004000 | Copied |

| Event log 0x04B000-0x06AFFF (992) | 0x2C0000-0x2DFFFF (992) | The 32 sectors copied in order (the same capacity, the same slots) |
| SSB patch 0x06B000-0x06EFFF | 0x008000-0x00BFFF | Copied (the firmware checks its SHA-256) |
| Trims 0x06F000 | 0x003000 | Copied |

Then the sector of layout 3 that holds layout 2's header (0x02C000, channel sector 28) last, then `STAMP` with layout 3: the radio restarts, checks every zone's count against its channels (CHAN-36) and comes up with the settings, slots, channels and lists as they were.
