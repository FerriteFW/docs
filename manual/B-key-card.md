## Appendix B. The key card

One fixed map; no key can be reassigned. Short = released under 700 ms. Long = held 700 ms. ▲▼ and the knob repeat at 10 per second after 400 ms.

| BACK | Up one level | Home, from anywhere | — | Up one level; cancel an edit |

**Hold a digit on home** (the shortcut runs at the long press, with a popup naming it; chapter 7, KEY-11):

| Key | Action | Key | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Home channel | 5 | Tones (the tone picker) |
| 1 | Band scope | 6 | Step: the next one (VFO only) |
| 2 | TX power: low, mid, high | 7 | Shift: simplex, +, − (on a channel: its editor) |
| 3 | Squelch (the value editor) | 8 | Offset (the value editor; on a channel: its editor) |
| 4 | Watch on/off | 9 | Copy: a VFO to a channel; a channel to the VFO (asks first) |

**Quick menu** (hold \* on home, HOME-17): a box over home while it is up. PTT A reverse, PTT B night red, PF1 GPS on/off, PF2 Bluetooth on/off, knob brightness, each at once; BACK, \* or any other key closes it (and does nothing else), as do 5 s idle. PTT A and PTT B never transmit while it is up, nor a PTT held through its close until let go; the side connector's PTT closes it and transmits.

**Scanning** (the scan screen): \* skips the hit for this scan, # skips it for every scan (a channel's Skip flag), ▲▼ set the direction and leave a hit, digits add zones, SIDE2 stops, PTT transmits on the hit (chapter 14, SCAN-16 – SCAN-19).

**Band scope:** ◀▶ marker, knob centre, OK tunes to the marker, # centres on the strongest, SIDE1/SIDE2 listen, \* mode, hold 1–9 / 0 trigger line, hold MENU options; ▲▼ and hold \* do nothing (chapter 21).

**FM broadcast:** ▲▼ or the knob tune by the step, ◀▶ seek, OK the saved stations (BCR-31), 1–4 its options, hold MENU options, BACK leaves, PTT leaves and transmits (chapter 20, BCR-18).

**HF mode** has no key or shortcut: MENU 0 3 4. Inside it: knob/▲▼ tune, ◀▶ band, \* mode, # recent list, hold MENU options, PTT transmits on 18–64 MHz where allowed (FM; AM is receive only), BACK leaves (chapter 20a).

**Text entry:** digits multi-tap; \* is a space; # cycles upper, lower, symbols; BACK deletes. There is no cursor: typing and BACK work at the end, and the knob and ◀▶ do nothing. Message text is predictive (TEXT-1 – TEXT-11): one press per letter, knob picks the word, \* or 0 space, 1 marks (. , ? ! ' / - @ :), hold \* spells; \* on the quick messages puts them away to write your own (TEXT-6.1).

**Key lock** (hold #, KEY-13): the keypad locks (PTT, SIDE1 and SIDE2 still work); within 3 s \* makes it All (PTT too) and 0 All but PTT (only PTT). Auto lock (after 0 9 1 1's time, KEY-20) locks the keypad. The volume knob and hold # always work (KEY-14).

**Setup:** OK next page, BACK previous, # skips the rest.
